## 🏆 Winning Scenarios for Each Mode

### 🔗 Best for Linked Mode:

1. **Strong Trending Markets**
   - Clear reversals at key levels
   - Major support/resistance bounces
   
2. **Higher Timeframes**
   - 4H, Daily charts
   - Swing trading approach

3. **Conservative Trading**
   - Capital preservation focus
   - Quality over quantity

---

### 🔓 Best for Independent Mode:

1. **Range-Bound Markets**
   - Multiple oscillations
   - Frequent stochastic signals

2. **Smaller Timeframes**
   - 5m, 15m, 1H charts
   - Scalping approach

3. **Active Trading**
   - More opportunities needed
   - Using additional confirmations
